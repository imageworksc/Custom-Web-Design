Discuss Your Website

AI, Template, or Custom-Built.

There are more ways than ever to build a website. The right approach depends on what the website needs to accomplish, how important it is to the business, and how much control you want over the final result.

Generated from a prompt

AI-Generated

Fast and cheap. AI can generate layouts, content, imagery, and code in minutes. But speed isn't strategy.

Without the direction of an experienced UX and design team, you get generic layouts, weak branding, and content that misses the mark. Fine for something simple, not for a business that needs to stand out, build trust, and win customers.

Content poured into fixed boxes

Template

Predictable, affordable, and easy to launch, but it works backwards. The layout is already built, so your content gets poured into boxes that were never designed for it.

In the AI era that compromise makes less sense, if technology can create original ideas faster than ever, there's less reason to start with the same design thousands of others can buy.

How ImageWorks Builds a Custom Website.

AI is a tool we use at every stage, it makes us faster, it doesn't make the decisions. Every stage runs on expert direction, whether that direction goes to AI or to our own designers and UX and art directors. The tool changes; the direction doesn't.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Plan Your Strategy and Goals

    First we get to know your company, your sales process, your revenue goals, who you need to reach, and what the website has to accomplish. Then we define what success looks like, decide how we'll measure it, and work out what every important page needs to do for the business.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Design Your Menu System

    Most businesses get this wrong, and it quietly costs them. Menus get labeled the way the company talks internally, divisions, service names, industry jargon, instead of the way customers actually search. We build an intent-based menu system: labels and sub-labels based on what people are genuinely looking for, not what the org chart says. That data exists now, so there's no reason to guess.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Create Your Content

    Once we know which pages your website needs, we improve your existing content or write it from scratch. Everything is built around your business, your customers, and what they're searching for.

    We use AI to help with research, drafting, and content analysis. But our experienced team makes the decisions, what to say, how to say it, and when to educate versus persuade. We refine every page to reflect your brand, connect with customers, and support rankings in Google and AI search.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Build Intelligent Wireframes

    AI helps us turn real content into structured page layouts in less time. From there, our UX team takes over, organizing each page into purposeful sections, headlines, services, benefits, trust signals, proof, and calls to action. Every layout is tailored to the business, its audience, and what the page needs to accomplish. Once the structure is approved, the wireframe goes to our design team, and that's where it starts becoming yours.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Custom Website Design System

    This is where the creative magic happens. Our designers take the approved wireframe and completely custom-design your homepage, every band, every detail, around your brand, not a template or AI-generated design. That homepage sets the standard for your design system: the colors, typography, imagery, visual elements, and responsive layouts that define how the whole site looks and feels, allowing us to build everything from simple pages to highly customized, complex experiences, depending on each page's importance and purpose.

  6. 6

    Step 6: Development & Launch

    AI assists with coding, testing, and repetitive production work. Our developers make the architectural decisions, handle integrations, control quality, and build the finished site into the right platform, connecting CRMs, analytics, forms, APIs, databases, and other business systems where needed.

Frequently Asked Questions About Custom Website Design

Do businesses still need a CMS now that AI can build websites?

Often, yes. A CMS like WordPress or Drupal lets you manage and update your content efficiently after launch. But the CMS doesn't determine how your site looks, the custom design does. AI can speed up how a site gets built; the CMS is still what you use to run it day to day.

What's the difference between an AI-built website and custom web design?

An AI-built website is generated quickly from a prompt, fast and affordable, but without strong direction it can feel generic. Custom web design starts with your business: strategy, content, UX, structure, design, development, search, and conversion shaped around what you actually need. We use AI to move faster, then our team makes the decisions that make the site yours.

What's the difference between a template and a custom website?

A template is someone else's design that you adapt your business to fit. A custom website is built around your brand, customers, and goals from the start. In the AI era, starting from the same design thousands of other businesses can buy makes less sense than it used to.

What does a custom website design system mean?

It's the visual language we create for your site, typography, color, layout, spacing, imagery, iconography, buttons and forms, animation, a core subpage system, and responsive behavior. Once the system exists, every page is built on that foundation, which keeps the site consistent, speeds up production, and puts design time where it matters most.

WordPress or Drupal, which one should I use?

It depends on the site's size, complexity, and how you want to manage it. WordPress is flexible and familiar; Drupal suits larger or more complex sites with advanced content management and backend needs. Either way, the CMS sits behind the finished site as the management engine, it doesn't dictate the design.

Do all the pages take the same amount of work?

No, and that's how we keep budgets sane. Every page is built from your design system, so nothing looks templated, but a straightforward subpage is a clean text and image layout that comes together quickly, while a page that carries real weight gets designed band by band with custom illustration, animation, and more involved storytelling. We put the heavy design time where it actually affects your brand and your revenue.

How does ImageWorks use AI when building websites?

We use AI throughout, research, planning, content development, wireframing, development assistance, and repetitive production work. It makes us faster and lets us explore more ideas. Then our team takes over, the decisions that require judgment, taste, and a real understanding of the business.

How much does custom web design cost?

It depends on the number of pages, how much custom design each needs, content, functionality, and integrations. Pages vary in complexity, so we put the heavy design time only where it matters, and AI reduces time across research, planning, and production. We give you a clear quote before any work begins.

How long does a custom website take to build?

Timeline depends on the size of the site, number of custom pages, functionality, and how quickly content and feedback come together. AI-assisted planning and production shorten the early phases. We give you a realistic schedule up front and keep the project moving through clear milestones.
