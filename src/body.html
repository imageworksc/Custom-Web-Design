Same Custom Website. Three Ways to Deploy and Manage It.

WordPress

A flexible, familiar CMS for content management, integrations, forms, SEO, and ongoing editing.

Drupal

A more advanced CMS for larger websites that need stronger content management, permissions, workflows, integrations, and backend functionality.

AI-Managed Newer

A newer approach where the website lives in an AI-managed environment rather than a traditional CMS, and many changes are made through prompts and AI-assisted development. It can offer a faster, more conversational way to manage and evolve a website.

For larger sites, a CMS may still be the better fit for managing pages, forms, files, schema, integrations, and backend systems. We support all three approaches and recommend the best fit for your site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is custom web design?

Custom web design is the process of designing and building a website from scratch around a specific business — its brand, goals, content, and audience — rather than using a pre-made template or theme.

At ImageWorks Creative, that means custom strategy, layouts, styles, and functionality created specifically for your business, so the finished website is unique to you and built to perform.

How much does a custom website cost?

Custom website pricing depends on the number of pages, level of custom design, content needs, functionality, integrations, and how the site is built and managed.

AI reduces time in research, planning, wireframing, and production, and standard pages help control costs. We price each project around the work it actually requires, and provide a clear quote before any work begins.

Why does custom web design cost more than a template?

A template is pre-built and reused across thousands of websites. A custom website is designed and built specifically for your business.

You're paying for strategy, original design, custom development, and functionality built around your goals — not a one-size-fits-all layout. The result is a site that looks distinct, converts better, and can scale as your business grows.

What's the difference between custom web design and a template?

A template gives you a fixed layout you adjust to fit your content. Custom web design does the opposite — the design is built around your brand, content, and conversion goals from the start.

Templates are faster and cheaper, but they look generic and limit flexibility. Custom websites are unique, more flexible, and built to perform.

Custom website vs. AI website builder — which is better?

AI website builders and generators are fast and inexpensive, and they can produce an acceptable site. What they can't do is deliver original, brand-specific design and the strategic thinking behind a high-performing website.

We use AI to accelerate research and production, then our creative team designs the site so it's genuinely yours — not another AI-generated look.

How long does it take to build a custom website?

Timeline depends on the size of the site, number of custom pages, functionality, integrations, and how quickly content and feedback come together. AI-assisted planning and production shorten the early phases.

We give you a realistic schedule up front and keep the project moving through clear milestones.

Do you build on WordPress, Drupal, or an AI-managed platform?

All three. The same custom website can be deployed in WordPress (a flexible, familiar CMS), Drupal (advanced content management and backend functionality for larger sites), or a newer AI-managed environment where changes are made through AI-assisted tools.

We recommend the best fit based on your site's size, content, and how you want to manage it.

Are your custom websites built for SEO and AI search?

Yes. Every custom site is structured for both traditional SEO and AI search from the ground up — clean architecture, metadata, schema, heading structure, and content organization that help search engines and AI systems like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Gemini understand and recommend your business.

It's the foundation our SEO and AI search programs build on.
